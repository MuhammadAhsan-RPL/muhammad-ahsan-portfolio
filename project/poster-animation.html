"A Smile is Charity" — Poster & Motion Animation

A visual storytelling project combining minimalist typography, grounded Islamic aesthetics, and gentle kinetic motion to communicate a timeless moral message with warmth and reverence.

Role

Visual & Motion Designer

Inspiration

Prophetic Hadith

Disciplines

Typography, Motion, Palette

Project Overview

Created as an academic motion design assignment, this project visually interprets the hadith: "Your smile in the presence of your brother is charity." through disciplined composition and kinetic rhythm.

The Challenge

Visualizing spiritual wisdom without resorting to cliché ornament or heavy decoration requires restraint. The challenge was ensuring the poster felt warm and sincere while keeping kinetic transitions calm rather than flashy.

The Solution

I curated an earthy palette—soft beige, desert amber, and deep garnet—coupled with centered serif typography and slow, breathing motion offsets that bring the static poster to life upon interaction.

Visual Design Pillars

Intentional Typographic Hierarchy

Carefully kerned and paced typography ensuring the core hadith text takes focal precedence.

Subtle Kinetic Drift Effects

Soft translation and opacity transitions delivering a calm, living feel on digital displays.

Source Attribution & Integrity

Authentic narrative referencing with meticulous Arabic typographic balance.

Multi-Format Deliverable

Formatted for print-ready resolution as well as vertical formats for modern digital media.

Design Retrospective

What I Learned

This project reminded me that design is communication first and foremost. Handling a meaningful spiritual quote pushed me to exercise restraint: using fewer elements, tuning contrast with precision, and using motion not for entertainment, but to amplify the serenity of the underlying thought. It honed my sensitivity toward pacing, composition, and emotional nuance.
