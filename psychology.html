M-Ahsan Psychology

Frontend Independent HIPAA

Overview

A professional multi-page website for a psychology practice. Built to establish trust, showcase credentials, and provide a calm, patient-centric experience. The site emphasizes accessibility and clear communication of services.

The Problem

Psychology practice needed a digital presence that reflects professionalism and builds trust with potential clients — without feeling cold or corporate.

The Solution

A responsive, multi-page website featuring service descriptions, therapist profiles, contact forms, and HIPAA compliance messaging. Clean design with warm tones to create a welcoming feel.

Key Features

  • Multi-page layout (Home, Services, About, Contact)
  • Fully responsive design
  • Contact form with validation
  • HIPAA compliance messaging
  • Trust signals & credentials display

Tech Stack

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript Responsive Design
