Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about my engineering workflow and approach.

Why focus on full-stack & mobile development?
Because I enjoy owning the complete lifecycle of a product—from designing the relational database schema and backend API to crafting the interface that users interact with on web and mobile.
How do you integrate AI tools into your workflow?
I use AI as an accelerator for research, rubber-duck debugging, and rapid prototyping—while keeping architectural decisions, database modeling, security, and code quality strictly under my own control.
How do you approach learning a new technology stack?
By reading official documentation, studying architectural patterns, and immediately building a functional project that solves a real problem.
Why do you care deeply about UI/UX design as a developer?
Because users experience the interface before they experience the backend architecture. A truly great digital product requires both rock-solid engineering and an intuitive, trustworthy user interface.
